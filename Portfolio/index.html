About

A developer who starts with the problem, not the framework.

I'm a Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, working toward my degree in 2028. What drew me to this field wasn't the syntax — it was the fact that a few hundred lines of code can make something genuinely easier for someone.

Most of my time goes into web development. I build full-stack applications with PHP and Laravel backed by MySQL, and I write the front end myself — semantic HTML, hand-authored CSS, and JavaScript that stays readable. I've built authentication systems, relational schemas with real constraints and transactions, and responsive interfaces that hold up on a phone as well as a laptop.

I care about problem-solving and I care about accessibility. Lumina — a tool helping people with visual or speech disabilities communicate — is the project I'm proudest of, because it forced me to design for people whose needs weren't my own. That habit carries into everything else I build.

Right now I'm deepening my JavaScript and React, sharpening my UI/UX practice, and looking for a junior role or internship where I can learn from people who've been doing this longer than I have.
